HOW IT WORKS

Energy stored in motion and pressure.

When renewable electricity is available, the Rambex system uses it to drive mechanical equipment and compress air for storage. When power is needed, stored energy is converted back into useful mechanical and electrical power.

System architecture and final specifications can be adapted for different applications and power requirements.
